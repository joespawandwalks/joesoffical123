About us

More Than Just a Walk. It's Personal Care.

Joe's Pawandwalks is a pet-care service in Ejipura, Bengaluru, built around trust, attention and genuine affection for dogs. Every pet is different, so we get to know yours and look after them the way you would.

  • TrustYou hand over your companion, so we earn that trust with steady, dependable care.
  • AttentionHabits, moods and routines matter. We notice them and care accordingly.
  • AffectionPatience, play and warmth are part of every visit.
  • ResponsibilityYour pet's safety and comfort come before everything else.
